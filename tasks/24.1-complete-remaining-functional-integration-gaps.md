Backend-only routes were classified rather than exposed incorrectly:
system/schema initialization is maintenance; Booking replay is diagnostics;
RAG search/ingestion primitives are consumed by AI/Knowledge workflows;
`/auth/me` is session support. Historical contact endpoints remain compatibility
storage, but active behavior is constrained to one current contact.

## Corrections

- Removed the Customer Profile multi-contact table. Contact name and phone are
  ordinary Customer detail rows on Manager and Customer profile screens.
- Removed dead Desktop contact list/deactivate/reactivate service operations.
- Removed all independent contact writes from Desktop; Customer and contact
  create/update now use one backend transaction and one Desktop request.
- Customer reads expose at most the primary/current active contact.
- Creating a second active contact or reactivating a historical contact beside
  the current one returns conflict and performs no write.
- Historical inactive rows remain stored; no destructive migration occurred.
- A contact-write failure cannot commit a Customer without its contact person.
- Verified Customer Booking notes already exist in create, edit, details, event,
  projection, and authorized response paths; no duplicate field was added.
